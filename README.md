# formet — version 3

The third design study for **formet**, the Manchester knitwear label, restyled after Zara and
Mertra at the owner's request: a white page with black type, a campaign hero and a clean
product grid. Versions 1 and 2: [Formet-](https://github.com/DM-tuition/Formet-) ·
[Formet2](https://github.com/DM-tuition/Formet2).

**Live:** https://dm-tuition.github.io/Formet3/

- **Campaign (Zara):** three photos side by side (one at a time on a phone), the wordmark across
  them. As you scroll it shrinks into the header while the white shop rises over the photos.
- **Shop (Mertra):** a ticker bar, a floating header, a four-wide grid of the three pieces on white
  plus an editorial tile; hover for the back view and a quick-add size row.
- **Product pages (Zara):** each piece opens full screen, with an image grid, sizes, details, care
  and delivery, and the other two pieces.
- **Kept from version 2:** the No. 1 dive through the back print with the glass lens, the circle
  that turns day into night, and the bag and three-step demo checkout.
- **Lookbook:** on a desktop the row slides sideways as you scroll; on a phone, swipe it.

This is not the official store. The bag and checkout are a demo: nothing is sold and no card
details are asked for. Photos are the brand's own.

## Version 4 (in `v4/`)

**Live:** https://dm-tuition.github.io/Formet3/v4/

A white site built on the owner's own marks: his wordmark, damask and woven tag (traced from his
files), and every product shot cut out and set on white. A short intro (the damask, then the
wordmark), the three pieces standing in front of a giant wordmark that docks into the header, the
ticker in the woven tag's colours, and smoother scroll animations on the No. 1 dive and the
Manchester banner.
